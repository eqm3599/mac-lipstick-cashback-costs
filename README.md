## Which MAC lipstick you're actually buying

The search term "mac lipstick" covers at least five different products, and the differences matter more than the shade name.

**M·A·Cximal Silky Matte** is the modernised version of the old Matte formula — MAC describes it as 12 hours of full-coverage colour with eight hours of moisture. It's the default choice for Ruby Woo and Velvet Teddy buyers, and it's the one most people mean when they say MAC lipstick.

**M·A·Cximal Sleek Satin** is the same $25 price with a creamier, glossier finish and less staying power. If matte formulas make your lips look like a raisin by hour four, this is the one to try.

**Powder Kiss Hazy Matte** is a different animal: soft-focus, blurred, hydrating for a claimed 10 hours. At $27 it's $2 more than the bullets above, and it looks nothing like a classic MAC matte — closer to a blotted stain.

**Powder Kiss Liquid Lipcolour** at $28 is the whipped liquid version of the same idea.

**Locked Kiss Ink 24HR** is the waterproof, transfer-proof matte liquid lip — the one you wear to a wedding where you're expected to eat and drink like a normal person. It's priced above the bullet range, and it's the only MAC lip product where transfer resistance is the entire point.

Then there are the Lustreglass Sheer-Shine lipsticks for people who want sheer colour and shine, and the classic Retro Matte for anyone still loyal to the driest finish MAC ever made.

None of this changes the price much. A finish doesn't earn you a discount. So let's talk about the part that does.

## The rules that decide whether your MAC cashback tracks

This is the section most cashback guides skip, and it's the reason people end up annoyed. MAC has some of the stricter tracking conditions among ShopBack merchants.

- **Clear your cart first.** ShopBack's MAC terms state plainly that items already in your MAC cart when you click through won't be tracked. Empty the cart, close the MAC app, then start from ShopBack and complete the purchase in one go.
- **Accept the cookies.** MAC can only confirm an order to ShopBack if you accept all cookies on their site. Block them at your peril.

The exclusion list changes; read the terms block on the offer page before checkout, not after.

Calculate cashback on the order amount excluding shipping, taxes, fees and service charges — not the gross total you see at checkout.

## How long the money takes, and where it goes

Cashback moves through three states: tracked (the retailer has seen the order), confirmed (the return window closed and the order is final), and withdrawable.

On MAC, tracked usually shows up within about two days. Confirmation runs to roughly 90 days in New Zealand and around 95 days in Hong Kong. Until it's confirmed, it isn't yours to withdraw — and if you return or exchange anything, that portion is clawed back.

Withdrawal thresholds differ by market. ShopBack's US payout guide states a US$5 minimum on confirmed cashback with no withdrawal fee, paid out by PayPal or ACH to a linked US bank account. Processing typically takes around 10 calendar days and can stretch longer during batch runs and holidays.

## Where cashback is the wrong answer

Three situations where you should ignore all of this:

**You want the lipstick today.** Cashback has no effect on what you pay at checkout. If you need a specific shade this afternoon, go buy it. Reading offer terms for 20 minutes to earn $1.88 later is a bad trade.

**You're buying one tube at a base rate.** At 3%, a $25 lipstick returns $0.75. That's not a saving; it's a rounding error with a 90-day delay attached.

**You found a better instant discount.** If MAC is running a sitewide sale and you've got a promo code from MAC's own channel, compare it against the cashback rate. You usually can't have both, because outside codes typically kill the tracking. A 15% instant discount beats a 7.5% cashback that arrives in three months.

Where it does make sense: multi-item orders, gift sets, restocking two or three formulas at once, and boosted campaign windows where the rate jumps into double digits.
